Restoration honesty · Runs in your browser

The original is the only evidence. What did the AI add?

AI upscalers and photo restorers fill in what the original never recorded — faces, text, textures. Some of it is plausible; none of it is proven. Invented Detail compares the restoration with the source and maps which details the source supports, which it contradicts, and which were invented.

Nothing is uploaded. Both images are compared inside this tab.

Four answers for every part of the picture

Faithful

The restoration matches the source and adds nothing the source could not show.

Enhanced

Sharper detail where the source already shows structure — plausible sharpening.

Invented

Fine detail where the source is flat or blurred. It may be right; the source cannot say.

Contradicted

Even blurred back to the source's resolution, the restoration disagrees with it.
