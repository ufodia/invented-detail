Restoration honesty · Runs in your browser

The original is the only evidence. What did the AI add?

AI upscalers and photo restorers fill in what the original never recorded — faces, text, textures. Compare the restoration with the source using a visual map of consistency and added detail. Transparent heuristics flag areas to review; they cannot prove whether a face, word or texture is historically correct.

Nothing is uploaded. Both images are compared inside this tab.

Four labels to guide your review

Faithful

Low difference and little added detail at the bounded analysis resolution.

Enhanced

Sharper detail where the source already shows structure — plausible sharpening.

Invented

Fine detail where the source is flat or blurred. It may be right; the source cannot say.

Contradicted

Even blurred back to the source's resolution, the restoration disagrees with it.
